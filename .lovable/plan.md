

# Civil Engineering Portfolio Website

A professional portfolio website to showcase your civil engineering expertise in **General Civil Engineering, Construction Management, Infrastructure, and Quantity Surveying**.

---

## 🏠 Home Page
- **Hero section** with your name, title, and a brief tagline about your expertise
- **Project Portfolio gallery** — showcasing your completed projects with images, descriptions, and categories (infrastructure, construction management, quantity surveying, etc.)
- **Quick overview** of your key skills and years of experience
- **Call-to-action** directing visitors to contact you

## 👤 About Me Page
- Your professional background, education, and certifications
- Areas of specialization (General Civil Engineering, Construction Management, Infrastructure, Quantity Surveying)
- Professional journey and career highlights

## 📄 Resume / CV Page
- Visual presentation of your experience, education, and skills
- Downloadable PDF option (you can upload your CV)

## ⭐ Testimonials Page
- Client reviews and feedback displayed in an attractive card layout

## 📞 Contact Page
- Contact form (name, email, subject, message) — displays on the page for visitors to fill out
- Your phone number, email address, and office location displayed alongside the form

---

## Design Style
- Clean, professional, and modern look fitting for an engineering business
- Responsive design that works on desktop and mobile
- Consistent color scheme with a trustworthy, professional feel (blues/grays)
